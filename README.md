**Credit Risk & Customer Segmentation Analysis | Power BI**

**Project Overview**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyse customer credit profiles, financial indicators and credit-score distribution.

The project explores customer segmentation, credit mix, occupation-wise credit profiles and loan distribution to support data-driven financial analysis.

**Business Objectives**

Analyse the distribution of Good, Poor and Standard credit scores.

Understand the relationship between credit mix and credit-score categories.

Compare credit-score distribution across occupations.

Examine customer distribution based on the number of loans.

Monitor important financial indicators such as income, outstanding debt and credit utilization.

**Key Insights**

Credit Score Distribution: Standard customers represent 51.88%, Poor customers 28.82%, and Good customers 19.30% of the displayed customer distribution.

Credit Mix Analysis: The dashboard compares credit-score composition across Good, Standard and Bad credit-mix categories.

Occupation Analysis: A matrix visual enables comparison of credit-score categories across occupations.

Loan Analysis: The dashboard examines customer distribution by number of loans.

These are descriptive findings from the dashboard and do not establish causal relationships.
